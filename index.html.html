Playoffs

The top 2 teams of each bracket (4 teams) go to the semifinals, one per court. Each bracket winner plays the runner-up of the other bracket (A1 vs B2 on Court 1, B1 vs A2 on Court 2). The two winners meet in the final, and the two losers play for 3rd place.

Pool play is 1–30. Playoffs are 31–32 (semifinals), 33 (3rd place) and 34 (final). Bracket and court fill in automatically and can be edited. The sheet prints on A4.

Clears all pool and playoff scores, team names, members and tiebreak bonuses, and cannot be undone. Click the button, then click it again to confirm.

Ranking: win–loss percentage first (wins divided by games played, so a 3–0 team ranks above a 3–1 team), then point differential (capped at ±9 per game, averaged per game so teams with fewer games are not penalized), then the tiebreak bonus (0–9), then fewest points allowed per game. The bonus only counts when win percentage and point differential are exactly equal, so for a head-to-head, three-way or sudden-death result, give the winning team a higher bonus. A bracket is complete after all 15 of its matches have a score. Semifinals are crossed between brackets: the winner of one bracket plays the runner-up of the other. Pool games go to 11, win by 2, cap 15; scores outside that are outlined in amber but still counted. Team names and members are edited in the Teams list (or click a name in the standings); a rename updates everywhere. Everything saves in this browser.
